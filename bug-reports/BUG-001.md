# BUG-001: Broken product images for problem_user

**Severity:** Medium
**Priority:** Medium
**Module:** Inventory
**Environment:** Chrome (latest), Windows 11 — https://www.saucedemo.com/inventory.html

## Preconditions
Logged in as `problem_user` / `secret_sauce`

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Navigate to https://www.saucedemo.com/
2. Log in with `problem_user` / `secret_sauce`
3. Observe product images on inventory page

## Expected Result
All 6 products display their correct product image

## Actual Result
Product images are broken/incorrect — same placeholder image used for multiple products, and some images do not match product names

## Evidence
See `evidence/screenshots/BUG-001.png`

## Impact
Users cannot visually identify products — affects purchase decision and trust
